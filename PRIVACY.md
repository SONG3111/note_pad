## English

### Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

Inspiration Notes is a fully offline, local application:

- All notes and to-dos you create are stored in a local database (`%APPDATA%\com.songqulv.notepad\`) on your device
- The app does not upload data over the network and contains no analytics or telemetry components
- There is no account system — we have no way to associate any data with you

### Data Storage and Deletion

- All data exists only on your own device
- After uninstalling the app, you can permanently remove all records by manually deleting the data directory above
- We (the developer) have no means to access, restore, or delete data on your device

### Permissions

- This app requests only the basic permissions required for a desktop app to run. It does not access your files, contacts, location, camera, or microphone

### Third Parties

- The app contains no ads, no third-party trackers, and no embedded browser data collection

### Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: <https://github.com/SONG3111/note_pad>
